Evviva

This project focused on developing a packaging system that balances structural integrity and consumer convenience with a bold brand narrative. The brand, 'Evviva'—an Italian exclamation meaning 'hurray'—is built on the belief that food is the ultimate celebration of life.

To reflect this, the design treats every meal not just as sustenance, but as a vibrant ceremony of flavor and connection, turning the packaging into a physical manifestation of a festive event.
