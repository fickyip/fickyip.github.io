Lumiwa

This project involves the creation of a bespoke lighting brand for a newly renovated accommodation in Keihoku, managed by ROOTS. Keihoku is a region defined by its natural abundance, with timber and agriculture at its heart.

Renowned for centuries for its exceptional wood, Keihoku remains a vital link between humanity and the environment—building a bridge from roots to branches, and from the forest to the community.
